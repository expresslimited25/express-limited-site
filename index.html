What the chatbot does

Answers common questions
Menu, prices, opening hours, parking and delivery, using only the information you give us.
Takes booking requests
It checks the date, repeats the details back to the customer, and saves confirmed requests for your team.
Speaks your customers’ language
English and Bahasa Malaysia, in a friendly tone that suits your business.
Asks your team when unsure
It says it will check, and asks for a contact number, instead of guessing.

How we set it up

  1. You send us the basics. Menu or product list, opening hours, prices and your rules.
  2. We write and test the chatbot. We try it with real questions, including Malay and mixed-language ones.
  3. It goes live on your website. We adjust it after we see real customer chats. Messaging app support is planned.

Who it is for

  • Cafes and restaurants that get the same questions every day
  • Online retail shops with repeat product and delivery questions
  • Other small businesses that want faster replies
